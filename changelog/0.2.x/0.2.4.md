# 0.2.4 — 2026-11-25

## Added

- **`euparl_get_plenary_votes` `agenda_items` mode** lists a sitting's published agenda in agenda order: debate, vote and urgency-vote items with `agenda_item_id`, `activity_type`, `label`, `heading`, `order`, `start_time`, `procedure_ids` and `document_ids`. Agendas are published from the 2024-12-16 sitting on, plus the weeks of 2023-07-10 and 2024-04-22; other sittings return an empty page with a notice. ([#33](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/33))
- **`euparl_get_plenary_votes` decision `breakdown`**: `breakdown: true` adds the recorded favor, against and abstention voters tallied by political group and by country as the roster for the sitting date publishes them, with `unmatched_mep_ids` for voters the roster lacks. A decision without all three recorded voter arrays, or an unreadable roster, returns a notice in place of `breakdown`. ([#46](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/46))
- **`euparl_search_documents` `mep_declarations` source** lists MEP conflict-of-interest declarations, filtered by the new `person_id` and `awareness_of_conflict` and by `title`, `related_process_id`, `year` and `term`. Rows and a `DCI-` get carry `awareness_of_conflict`, `conflict_description`, `capacities` and version links; `text`, sitting dates, and `term` with `person_id` are rejected with `invalid_filter`. ([#34](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/34))

## Changed

- **`euparl_search_speeches` without `text`** returns at most 400 rows per call: a larger `limit` is normalized to 400 with a notice and `next_offset`, where 0.2.3 bounded a call at 10,000 rows and a large `limit` could end in a timeout with no rows. With `EUPARL_CLAMP_LIMITS=false`, `limit` is still accepted up to 10000. ([#51](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/51))
- **Tool descriptions** are shorter across the nine tools; the advertised input and output structure is unchanged. ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/49))
- **`eu-parliament-guide` skill** (1.4) covers agenda items, decision breakdowns and MEP declarations.

## Fixed

- **Reissued document files and multi-title records** failed with a serialization error when Parliament published several `issued`, `byteSize` or title values. Document, question and adopted-text reads now answer: a link with several values carries `issued_values` or `size_bytes_values`, and a `title` adds `values`, in published order. ([#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/50))
- **A dated roster that publishes a null political group or country** reads that field as absent, where 0.2.3 failed the whole roster.
- **A procedure cross-reference that is not a procedure identifier** is left out of `procedure_ids` with a notice naming it, where 0.2.3 failed the call; four MEP declarations publish such values. Other identifier kinds stay strict ([#54](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/54)).
