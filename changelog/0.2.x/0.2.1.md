# 0.2.1 — 2026-11-02

## Added

- **`euparl_get_plenary_votes` `vote_item_id`** (decisions mode) selects every decision of one vote item, such as a procedure's `PLENARY_VOTE_RESULTS` event; the vote item's date must match `sitting_id`. ([#19](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/19))
- **Decision rows** carry `partial_document_ids` and `vote_item_ids`; `document_id` also matches part-of relations. When it leaves out decisions of a vote item it matched, a notice names that vote item. A decisions walk continued with a 0.2.0 snapshot returns `snapshot_changed` once because rows gained these fields; restart at offset 0. ([#19](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/19))
- **`euparl_list_procedures` `snapshot`** is an optional input with `process_type`; a changed set fails `snapshot_changed`, and without `process_type` it fails `invalid_mode_fields`. ([#23](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/23))

## Changed

- **Decisions pages** end at whole decisions before their rows render past 92,000 characters, with a notice; `page.limit` stays the requested limit and `next_offset` resumes at the first decision left out. ([#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/18))
- **`euparl_list_procedures` with `process_type`** reads the complete set in one request, sorts by `procedure_id` and pages it locally, so a walk returns each procedure once at any `limit`; `upstream_paging_stalled` is the reason when the acquisition is incomplete. Unfiltered pages keep upstream paging and carry a notice that Parliament serves two orderings, so pages can repeat or omit procedures. ([#23](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/23))
- **`euparl_search_documents` get** on adopted texts, plenary documents and committee documents makes a second family read for `procedure_ids`; if it fails, the generic record is returned with a notice that `procedure_ids` may be incomplete. ([#20](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/20))
- **`euparl_list_reference` organizations** accepts committee, group and term codes (`org/IMCO`, `org/S-D`, `org/ep-9`, `org/EU_PARLIAMENT`), case preserved; a miscased code is `found: false`. ([#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/21))
- **Term notices**: an empty speech search names the term searched and the term dates, and says to pass the term containing `sitting_date` or, without one, `term` for the other term; unresolved voter names say `roster_term` must be the term containing the sitting date. ([#22](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/22))
- **Empty-decisions notice**: a sitting that published no decisions reports "No decision data was published for this sitting" whatever the filters; a sitting whose decisions publish no relations points to the report citation in decision labels. ([#19](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/19))
- **Empty pages past offset 0** say so by position rather than claiming no match.
- **`eu-parliament-guide` skill** (1.1) covers the vote item route to a final vote, organization codes, `procedure_ids` on documents, and the term-9 speech start in July 2021.

## Fixed

- **`euparl_get_plenary_votes` sittings** from 2014 to 2019 return `date`; every `activity_date` read accepts the typed-literal form Parliament publishes for those years. ([#26](https://github.com/cyanheads/eu-parliament-mcp-server/issues/26))

## Dependencies

- **tsc-alias** ^1.9.9 → ^1.9.10.
